# KiloClaw: A Revolutionary New Tool for AI Deployment
![Blog Image](/static/images/KiloClaw_20260225022652.png)



Kilo has officially launched KiloClaw, a managed service that allows users to deploy OpenClaw agents in a matter of seconds. This new offering is positioned to streamline the deployment process for both developers and non-technical professionals, aiming to make AI accessible to a wider audience.

## What is KiloClaw?

KiloClaw provides a fully managed environment for running OpenClaw, which is recognized as one of the most popular open-source AI agents globally. With KiloClaw, users can deploy an OpenClaw agent without the hassles of server provisioning, configuration, or maintenance. This service allows users to start running their AI agents in under a minute, making it an attractive option for those looking to integrate AI into their workflows quickly.

## Key Features of KiloClaw

### One-Click Deployment

KiloClaw differentiates itself with its one-click deployment feature. Users simply need to create a Kilo account, and they can start deploying agents immediately. This ease of use is crucial for democratizing access to AI capabilities, moving beyond developers to include enterprise managers and non-technical users.

### Continuous Updates

One of the standout aspects of KiloClaw is its commitment to maintaining up-to-date software. Unlike other services that fork the original code, KiloClaw hosts the actual OpenClaw version. This ensures that as OpenClaw evolves, KiloClaw users receive automatic updates without needing to execute manual operations, such as pulling updates from a git repository.

### Extensive Model Library

KiloClaw boasts access to over 500 AI models, allowing users to experiment with various functionalities and find the models that best suit their needs. This feature is particularly beneficial for enterprises looking to utilize different AI capabilities across their operations.

### Transparent Pricing

KiloClaw operates on a transparent pricing model with zero markup on AI tokens. Users can leverage their existing Kilo Gateway credits, ensuring a seamless billing experience without any unexpected costs.

## The Importance of Community and Open Source

Despite being a paid service, KiloClaw embraces an "open core" philosophy. The underlying Kilo CLI and core extensions are MIT-licensed, allowing for community auditing. This transparency is vital for security-conscious enterprises who need assurance that the tools they are using are secure and reliable.

## Innovations to Support OpenClaw Users

In conjunction with KiloClaw, Kilo has introduced PinchBench, an open-source benchmark designed specifically for evaluating real OpenClaw workflows. This tool assists users in determining which models work best for their specific applications, enhancing the overall usability of the OpenClaw platform.

## Conclusion: A Step Towards an Agentic Future

With the launch of KiloClaw, Kilo aims to make AI capabilities more accessible and manageable for a broad range of users. The focus on ease of use, continuous updates, and a robust library of models positions KiloClaw as a significant player in the crowded market of AI deployment solutions.
