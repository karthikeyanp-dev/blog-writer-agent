# Samsung Integrates Perplexity into Galaxy AI for Enhanced User Experience

Samsung is taking a significant step in enhancing its Galaxy AI system by integrating Perplexity as an additional AI agent for its upcoming S26 series flagship devices. This integration aims to provide users with more flexibility, choice, and a seamless experience across various applications.

## A New Wake-Up Call: "Hey Plex"

One of the notable features of this new integration is the dedicated wake phrase, "Hey Plex." Users will be able to easily initiate the Perplexity AI agent either by voice command or through quick-access controls, such as pressing and holding the side button of their devices. This accessibility is designed to ensure that contextual assistance is readily available whenever it's needed.

## Embedded Across Samsung Apps

Perplexity will be deeply integrated into several Samsung applications, including Samsung Notes, Clock, Gallery, Reminder, and Calendar. Additionally, it will support select third-party apps, although specific names have yet to be disclosed. This system-level embedding allows for smoother, multi-step workflows, enabling users to transition between tasks without the constant need to switch apps or repeat commands.

## A Cohesive AI Ecosystem

Samsung's approach to Galaxy AI is to create a multi-agent ecosystem that orchestrates different forms of AI into a single, cohesive experience. According to Won-Joon Choi, President and COO of Samsung's Mobile eXperience Business, this development reflects their commitment to building a rich, open, and integrated AI ecosystem. Users will have greater control over how they complete complex tasks, allowing for a more natural interaction with their devices.

## Enhancing Everyday Tasks

The integration of Perplexity is part of Samsung's broader vision to reduce the effort and steps required for everyday tasks. With insights indicating that nearly 80% of users rely on multiple AI agents for various tasks, this evolution of Galaxy AI is timely. By providing a choice of integrated agents, Samsung aims to cater to the diverse preferences and routines of its users.

## Looking Ahead

As Samsung prepares for its Galaxy Unpacked event, more details about the integration of Perplexity and its functionalities are expected to be revealed. This development not only enhances the capabilities of Samsung's flagship devices but also signals a shift in how users will interact with AI in their daily lives.
